FAQ

Questions, answered

When does Tamish launch?

We're getting ready for launch on iPhone and Android. Join the waitlist and we'll email you once, on the day the app is available in your country.

How does the Benefits Finder know what applies to us?

You choose your country and region, and Tamish matches the schemes there against your family profile: children's ages, household income band and work status. Each scheme shows “likely”, “possibly” or “unlikely”, with the reasons and a link to the official source. It's guidance, not a decision: the official body that runs the scheme always makes the final call.

Is the benefits information kept up to date?

Every entry links to its official government source and shows when it was last checked. Entries are reviewed on a regular schedule and after budget announcements, and each page has a button to report anything that looks wrong.

What's free, and what needs Premium?

The calendar, lists, chores and the benefits guide with eligibility checks are free, with no ads and no member limit. Premium adds value estimates, deadline and renewal reminders, claim tracking, unlimited captures and calendar sync, for one price that covers the whole household.

Does everyone in the family need to install the app?

No. Grandparents, sitters and co-parents can receive tasks and confirm events from a text-message link, with no app and no account.

What do you do with our family's data?

We use it to run Tamish for you, and nothing else. We don't show ads, and we never sell or share your family's information. Tamish is paid for by the optional Premium subscription.

Can we bring our calendar from another app?

Yes. Any app that exports a calendar feed (ICS) can be imported in one step, and lists can be brought over with a guided import.
