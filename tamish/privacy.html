Tamish is a family organizer. Families trust us with information about their children and their home life, so we keep this simple: we collect as little as we can, we never show ads, and we never sell or share your information for advertising.

This policy covers this website and the launch waitlist. The Tamish app will have its own privacy policy, published before the app is available, which will explain how calendar, family and benefits information is handled inside the app.

Who we are

This website is run by [Company legal name], [registered address] (“Tamish”, “we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the personal information described here.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you join the waitlist.
  • Basic technical information that any web server receives when you load a page, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. Our hosting provider, [hosting provider], keeps these in short-lived server logs for security.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't run analytics that follow you across other websites. If we add privacy-friendly analytics later, we will update this page first.

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.

How we use it

  • To send you one email when Tamish launches in your country, and, only if you agree, occasional product news.
  • To keep the website secure and working.

Our legal basis is your consent for the waitlist (you can withdraw it at any time) and our legitimate interest in running a secure website for server logs.

We never sell your information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the site, under contracts that let them use it only on our behalf:

  • [Form or email service], which stores the waitlist and sends the launch email.

We keep your waitlist email until Tamish launches in your country and for up to 12 months after, or until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. Server logs are deleted after [30] days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under UK or EU data protection law, or state privacy laws in the US), you can ask us to:

  • show you the information we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • stop using it, or restrict how we use it
  • give you a copy in a portable format

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. To make any other request, contact us using the details below. We will reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority, such as the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK.

Children

This website and the waitlist are for adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.
