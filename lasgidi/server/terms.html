What you buy

Cosmetics: name-tag colours, avatar accessories and house paint. They never give in-game naira, skills, status or any advantage, and they are not part of any life's score or replay.

Wallet top-ups: game money added once to the life you are playing. Game money has no cash value, cannot be withdrawn or transferred, and is not refundable once added. A life that takes a top-up is marked Boosted and can never appear on the Hall of Fame or the weekly board.

Who can buy

You must be 18 or over. Payments are handled by Flutterwave (in naira) or PayPal (in US dollars). This game never sees or stores your card details.

Your items

Items are tied to the shop key stored on this device. Clearing the browser's site data removes the key; keep your receipt reference, and the shop owner can restore your items from it.

Refunds

If a payment went through but the item did not unlock, or you were charged twice, contact the shop owner with your receipt reference within 14 days for a refund. Digital items you have already received are otherwise non-refundable, except where the law says otherwise.
